1. Pilot Scope & Participating Parties

This Proof of Concept (PoC) establishes a 90-day pilot between the Platform Provider and the participating Outpatient Facility / Medical Plaza and its Resident Medical Consultants. During the pilot, the facility and consultants receive full access to the Hub-and-Spoke scheduling platform, including multi-clinic rules, travel buffers, WhatsApp/email reminders, smart waitlists, and Kenya Data Protection Act (KDPA) consent tools.

Signing this agreement gives the facility and its consultants access to the platform. It does not start the pilot period: each consultant's 90 days are counted from their own first booking, as set out in section 2.

2. Three-Month PoC Rollout Schedule

Consultant cancellation charge New

Ksh. 100 is added to the consultant's monthly invoice for each booking cancelled by the consultant or by the facility's front-desk staff on the consultant's behalf. The consultant is notified by email each time the charge is applied. The charge does not apply when the cancellation is made at the patient's request, or when the facility is closed for the day. It applies from Day 91 of the consultant's pilot, under both plans.

Active consultants and invoices New

  • An active consultant is one with at least one appointment on the platform in the calendar month.
  • Invoices are paid by M-Pesa: an STK Push is sent to the consultant's phone. The Platform Provider may also record a payment made by other means.
  • Unpaid invoices are reminded by email and WhatsApp. If an invoice is still unpaid 14 days after its due date, patients can no longer book that consultant online until it is paid. Bookings made by facility staff are not affected.

4. Cash vs. Insurance Patient Handling & Safeguards

  • 100% Clinical Revenue Retention: The Ksh. 250 booking convenience fee is collected directly by the platform as a digital slot-reservation fee. It is never deducted from the consultant's clinical fee or the hospital payout, except where a consultant chooses to absorb it for insured patients as set out below.
  • Cash (Self-Pay) Patients: Patients pay the Ksh. 250 convenience fee via M-Pesa STK Push when raising an advance booking, and settle the consultant's full consultation fee directly at the clinic upon arrival.
  • Same-day walk-ins: Changed Same-day physical walk-ins at reception are exempt from the fee. A facility may choose to charge them by notifying the Platform Provider in writing. Same-day bookings made online or through a booking link sent by the clinic are advance bookings and carry the fee.
  • Insurance Patients (SHA, Jubilee, Britam, AAR, CIC, etc.): Patients declare "Insurance" and submit insurer details at booking so front-desk staff can run Slade360/Smart pre-authorization before arrival. The Ksh. 250 fee is never billed onto insurance claim forms. Changed Each consultant chooses one of:
    1. insured patients pay the Ksh. 250 out of pocket via M-Pesa, as cash patients do; or
    2. insured patients pay nothing upfront, and the consultant absorbs the Ksh. 250 from their own consultation fee. Absorbed fees are billed on the consultant's monthly invoice from Day 31 of their pilot, and follow the same cancellation rules as fees paid by patients (section 5).
  • Once a day: New A patient pays at most one booking fee per calendar day, across all consultants on the platform.

5. Incentives, Cancellations & Refunds

  • Front-Desk Incentive Rules & Anti-Gaming: The Ksh. 50 front-desk reward is held as pending and accrues only when the appointment is completed (status = completed), including bookings whose fee the consultant absorbs. It is disbursed weekly via M-Pesa. Cancelled or no-show appointments do not trigger staff payouts. New Incentives are subject to review: the Platform Provider may withhold an incentive on a booking flagged as irregular, such as a booking cancelled and remade for the same patient.
  • Fair Cancellation & Refund Policy:
    • If a consultant or facility cancels a clinic session, affected patients automatically receive a free rebooking credit, or a full Ksh. 250 M-Pesa refund if they ask for one. An absorbed fee is not billed to the consultant.
    • Patient cancellations more than 24 hours ahead transfer freely to a new slot.
    • New Patient cancellations less than 24 hours ahead, and no-shows, are not refunded. An absorbed fee is billed to the consultant.
  • Consultant cancellation charge: as set out in section 3.
  • Leaving the platform: New If the facility leaves the pilot or the platform, patients' unused credits are refunded by M-Pesa. Earned front-desk incentives are paid; pending incentives are paid only for appointments completed before the exit date.
  • Patient Booking Terms: New Patients accept the terms in Schedule A when they book. The facility agrees that these terms are shown to its patients.

6. Data Privacy & Ownership (KDPA)

All patient consent logs, masked contact records, and facility audit trails comply with the Kenya Data Protection Act. The facility and consultants retain full ownership of their clinical schedules and patient records.

New Patients' phone numbers and email addresses are masked in lists, boards, search results and exports. Staff see them in full only when they open an appointment they are permitted to handle, and each such view is recorded in the audit trail. Appointments at other facilities show only as busy, without patient details.

Schedule A: Patient Booking Terms

Shown to patients, and accepted by them, when they book an appointment that carries the booking fee.

  1. A Ksh. 250 slot reservation fee is paid by M-Pesa when you book an appointment in advance. It holds your slot and is separate from the consultation fee, which you pay at the clinic.
  2. You pay at most one booking fee per day, however many appointments or consultants you book that day.
  3. If you reschedule more than 24 hours before your appointment, your fee moves to the new time.
  4. If you cancel less than 24 hours before your appointment, or do not attend, the fee is not refunded.
  5. If the consultant or the facility cancels, you receive a free rebooking credit, or a full refund of the fee if you ask for one.
  6. Same-day walk-ins at reception do not pay the fee, unless the facility has chosen to charge it. Bookings made online or through a link from the clinic pay it, even for the same day.
  7. The fee is never added to an insurance claim. If you are insured, your consultant decides whether you pay it.
